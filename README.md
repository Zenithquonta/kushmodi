## Observatory

<details>
<summary><strong>Open the astronomy mission log</strong></summary>

I'm Kush Modi, an Integrated MBA Tech (Computer Engineering) student in Mumbai, on a five-year dual degree at MPSTME, NMIMS (2024 to 2029, expected). I'm an avid amateur astronomer, and that led to a telescope mount project and an interest in autonomous systems for unmapped environments. The artwork here starts from my own telescope-under-the-Milky-Way photograph.

**Automated alt-azimuth star tracker and mount**

A low-cost two-axis alt-az tracking mount with stepper motors and custom reduction gearing designed in Autodesk Fusion 360. Open-source ephemeris data, smartphone IMU feedback and microcontroller control give real-time sky-coordinate compensation and object tracking.

**Team Astrofix, Smart India Hackathon 2025**

As team leader, I led a cross-functional team through ideation, technical architecture and rapid deployment for a national-priority problem statement (Ministry of Education Innovation Cell). I managed sprints, documentation, architecture reviews and presentations. We qualified and competed at the national level.

AstroFixxer repositories: [AstroFixxer web](https://github.com/Zenithquonta/astrofixer-baby) · [AstroFixxer Android](https://github.com/Zenithquonta/astrofixxer-android)

</details>

## Flight deck

<details>
<summary><strong>Open the flight deck</strong></summary>

Aviation, spacecraft and exploration pull at me. Star Trek and Star Wars inspire the ships in the artwork.

**NETRA (Navigation and Exploration in Terrain-Restricted Areas)**

A modular autonomous aerial exploration platform for GPS-denied, communication-constrained, unmapped terrain. A secondary pathway leads toward hazardous-environment work such as mining and disaster response.

**ESP32-S3 micro drone (in design)**

I'm designing a sub-100 g drone with coreless motors, flown over WiFi through WebSocket control. The plan includes PID stabilisation, an on-board vision layer for object tracking, and IMU-based dead reckoning.

**Exploration beyond engineering**

High-altitude trekking certification (Beas Kund expedition), intermediate snow skiing certification (Gulmarg), and PADI Scuba Diver certification in progress.

</details>

## Fab lab

<details>
<summary><strong>Step inside the maker workshop</strong></summary>

Making is the other constant: imagining something, building it, and finding out what it can do.

- **CAD:** Autodesk Fusion 360 and SolidWorks, with parametric 3D modelling.
- **Fabrication:** FDM printing on Bambu Lab and Creality Ender printers in PLA, PETG and TPU, plus aluminium extrusion, sheet metal and acrylic.
- **Electronics:** I design my own PCBs. Embedded work with Arduino, STM32, ESP32-S3, ESP32-C3, PID control, WebSocket, ESP-NOW and motor drivers.
- **LEGO:** a hobby, and a good way to think in small pieces.

Look for the rocket on the printer, the rotating wireframe display, the brick-built spacecraft, and the rover outside the workshop.

</details>

## Mission log

<details>
<summary><strong>Projects, tools, and milestones</strong></summary>

**Robotics: Team Darwin, MPSTME**

- **Head Design and Simulation Engineer (2025 to 2026):** I built the Gazebo simulation for the autonomous ground vehicles Vega and Kaizen (Intelligent Ground Vehicle Competition, IGVC), so navigation could be tested before hardware runs. I designed the rover chassis in aluminium extrusion, steel, acrylic and custom 3D-printed parts, balancing rigidity and weight for off-road use. I also originated the dynamic cost-field navigation approach, with a seven-rule priority hierarchy for obstacle avoidance and lane following, which the software team implemented.
- **Electronics core member (2024 to 2025):** I integrated the ZED 2i, RPLidar A3, uBlox GPS, MPU-9250, Arduino Mega and Rhino motor drivers, with low-latency ROS serial links for motor actuation and sensor telemetry.
- **Team results:** 1st in qualifying and 5th in the AutoNav challenge at IGVC.

**Tools I work with**

Python · C · C++ · Java · SQL · JavaScript · ROS · Gazebo · sensor fusion · SLAM · path planning · LiDAR · stereo vision · IMU · React · Node.js · REST APIs · Supabase · Google Cloud Platform · Git · Linux · Docker

I also build with AI-assisted tools: Cursor, Claude and GitHub Copilot.

</details>
